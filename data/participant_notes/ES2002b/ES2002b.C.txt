Functions to be included were decided to be +/- channel and volume, power (standby) and the usual numerical keypad to skip between channels.
It was decided to include an extra drawer, not directly accessible, to hold any extra funcitons we decide to unclude that wouldn't be part of the basics.
Command groupings were decided: Audio (eg mute, surround sound), Video (eg colour, contrast) and Device (eg Standby, channel +/-)
