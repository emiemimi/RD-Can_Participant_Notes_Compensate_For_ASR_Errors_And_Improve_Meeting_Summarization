The kick-off meeting for the remote control project was held, attended by the project manager, industrial designer, user interface designer, and marketing expert.
The team discussed user needs and potential advantages over competitors, including originality, trendiness, and user-friendliness.
The design process will be divided into three stages: functional design, conceptual design, and detailed design.
The remote control should be waterproof, have a small number of buttons, and be able to withstand shocks.
A unique idea proposed was a motion-tracking feature and selling a protective case with the remote control.
The team agreed to divide tasks and responsibilities, with the industrial designer focusing on industrial design, user interface designer working on user interface, and marketing expert researching market requirements.
The next meeting was scheduled for 30 minutes later.
