A meeting was held to discuss the design of a new remote control.
The team aimed to create a product that is original, trendy, and user-friendly.
The design process will be divided into three main points: functional design, conceptual design, and data design.
Functional design will involve identifying user needs and technical functions, while conceptual design will specify the components and user interface.
Data design will consist of implementing and detailing the design choices made during the conceptual design phase.
The team brainstormed ideas, such as making the remote control waterproof, adding a special button on the TV, and incorporating a whistle feature.
They also discussed the importance of having a low cost and a high selling price to target a profit of 50 million euros.
The team decided to have the industrial designer, user interface designer, and market expert work on the project, and the next meeting was scheduled for 30 minutes later.
The team also agreed to think about the design ideas and requirements discussed during the meeting.
