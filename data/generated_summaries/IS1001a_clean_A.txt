The kick-off meeting for the new remote control project discussed user needs, originality, and trends.
The team wants to create a user-friendly, trendy, and original remote control that has a lower production cost and higher selling price to target a significant profit.
They considered features such as multiple device control, minimal buttons, and water-proofing.
The team also explored the idea of incorporating a whistle or sound feature to locate the remote control when lost.
The next steps involve the industrial designer researching the design requirements, the user interface designer working on the interface and functionality, and the marketing expert identifying absolute requirements for the remote control.
The team will continue to work on the project and receive updates on the next steps.
