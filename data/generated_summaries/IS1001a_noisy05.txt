The meeting focused on the design process of a new remote control.
The team discussed the importance of being original and trendy to compete with competitors, while also being user-friendly.
They broke down the design process into three main points: functional design, conceptual design, and data design.
The team brainstormed ideas for the remote control, including features such as a waterproof design, shock resistance, and a unique whistle remote control.
They also discussed the importance of including a backlight and a special button on the TV to prevent loss of the remote control.
The team assigned tasks to each member, including the industrial designer to find out more about industrial design and the user interface designer to think about convenient features for the user.
The meeting concluded with a brief discussion about the next steps and a review of the tasks assigned to each member.
