The meeting was the kickoff for the remote control project, where a team consisting of a project manager, industrial designer, user interface designer, and marketing expert discussed the design requirements for a new remote control.
The team aimed to create a product that is original, trendy, and user-friendly, with a design process divided into three main points: functional design, conceptual design, and design.
The team discussed ideas on the number of devices the remote control can control, the need for a water-proof design, and the possibility of adding a whistle or audio recording feature.
The project manager assigned tasks to the team members, including finding out more about industrial design costs and researching user requirements.
The next meeting was scheduled in 30 minutes.
