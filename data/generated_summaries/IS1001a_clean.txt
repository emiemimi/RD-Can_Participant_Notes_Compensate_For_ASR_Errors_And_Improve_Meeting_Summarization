The meeting focused on designing a new remote control for a company, aiming to be original, trendy, and user-friendly.
The design process will be divided into three main points: functional design, conceptual design, and desired design.
The goal is to produce a remote control with a production cost lower than €12.50 and a selling price of twice that price, targeting a profit of €50 million.
The team discussed various features, such as the number of buttons, lighting, and waterproofing, with some suggestions for innovative features like whistle-tracking and a special button to locate the remote control.
The next steps will involve Industrial Design, User Interface Design, and Market Expert input.
The team will receive further advice and guidance to inform their design decisions.
The meeting concluded with a plan to reconvene in 30 minutes to continue the discussion and assign tasks to each team member.
