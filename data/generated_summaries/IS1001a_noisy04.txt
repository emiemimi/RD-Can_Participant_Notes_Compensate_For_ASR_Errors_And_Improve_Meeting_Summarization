The meeting discussed the design of a new remote controller for a company.
The goal is to create an original and user-friendly product that gives the company an advantage over competitors.
The design process will be divided into three main points: functional design, conceptual design, and design design.
The team will identify user needs, technical functions, and user interface elements.
They also discussed various ideas for the remote control, such as being waterproof, having a whistle or a special button on the TV, and having a motion tracking feature.
The team decided to bundle these features together and sell them as a single product.
The next meeting is scheduled in 30 minutes, and the team will continue to work on the design and functionality of the remote control.
