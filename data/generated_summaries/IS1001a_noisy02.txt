The meeting discussed the design of a new remote control to gain an advantage over competitors.
The design process was divided into three main points: functional design, conceptual design, and detailed design.
The team brainstormed ideas for the design, including a unique feature to prevent losing the remote control, such as a whistle or audio recording capabilities.
They also discussed the importance of the remote control being user-friendly, original, and trendy.
The team agreed to have a waterproof remote control and one that can resist various shocks.
They also suggested adding a feature to track the remote control, making it easier to find if lost.
The next steps were assigned to different team members, including an industry designer, user interface designer, and market expert, to research and provide advice on the project.
The meeting concluded with a plan to reconvene in five minutes to continue brainstorming and discussing ideas for the remote control design.
