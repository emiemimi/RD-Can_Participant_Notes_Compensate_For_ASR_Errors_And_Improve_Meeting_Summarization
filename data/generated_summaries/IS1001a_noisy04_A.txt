A kickoff meeting was held for the remote control project to discuss the design process.
The team discussed the importance of being original, trendy, and user-friendly to gain an advantage over competitors.
The design process will be divided into three main stages: functional design, conceptual design, and design implementation.
The team brainstormed ideas for the remote control's design, including a waterproof feature, a whistle remote cover, and an audio recording function.
The next steps will be to research the market requirements and assign tasks to each team member.
A follow-up meeting is scheduled in 30 minutes to continue the discussion.
The team consists of a project manager, industrial designer, user interface designer, and marketing expert.
