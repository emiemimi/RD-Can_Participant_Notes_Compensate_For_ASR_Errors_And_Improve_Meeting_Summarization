The team discussed the design of a new remote control for the kick-off meeting, focusing on user needs and advantages over competitors.
They identified three main design steps: functional, conceptual, and design design.
Key points discussed included the need for originality, trendiness, and user-friendliness.
Ideas for the remote control's features, such as being waterproof, having a shock-resistant design, and being able to control multiple devices, were also discussed.
The team decided to consider adding features like a tracking system and audio recording capabilities.
The project manager assigned tasks to each team member, including researching industry costs, user interface design, and market expert feedback.
The next meeting was scheduled for 30 minutes, where they would discuss further design ideas and requirements.
